1 in 4 people in the US have a treatable mental health condition, but most providers don't accept insurance, making therapy too expensive for most people. Headway’s mission is to fix this by building a new mental healthcare system everyone can access. We started by solving the biggest barrier to care: insurance. The admin work - credentialing, claims, payment reconciliation - is a nightmare. We've automated that.

But we're going further. Over 75,000 providers across all 50 states run their practice on our software, serving over 1 million patients. We are building the best tools for therapists to run their entire practice, reimagining the experience of finding a therapist, and investing in the platform foundations to enable this at scale. We aren't just a billing layer; we are becoming the platform where care actually happens.

We're a Series D company with $325M+ in funding (a16z, Accel, Spark Capital, etc.), looking for exceptional people to help us achieve this mission. We want your time here to be the most meaningful experience of your career. Join us, and help change mental healthcare for the better.

THE WORK

Backend at Headway involves high-stakes logic. You’re building a reliable abstraction layer over a fragmented healthcare system. What you could work on:

 - The Modern AI-enabled Clinical System: Help design and build the systems that store and manage clinical data. This isn't just a database; it's a high-integrity system for treatment plans, progress notes, patient history, and AI-powered clinical intelligence - from session transcript-to-note generation to automated summaries that give providers their time back.

 - The Insurance Billing Engine: We apply AI pragmatically (blog https://medium.com/headway-tech/improving-healthcare-billing-with-machine-learning-ba3446979d91) to reduce administrative burdens for providers and handle the burden of traditional billing workflows. You’ll scale the systems that handle the unique, often nonsensical rules of every major insurance payer to accurately estimate and file claims hundreds of thousands of times per month. 

 - Patient-First Infrastructure: We’re moving toward a patient-centric model. Build the backend services that power personalized care recommendations, health tracking, and longitudinal data views as well as innovative user experiences to help patients find a therapist that meets their needs

 - Financial Integrity: Work on the ledger systems that ensure 70,000+ providers get paid accurately. This requires building for idempotency and strict consistency in a multi-party marketplace.
