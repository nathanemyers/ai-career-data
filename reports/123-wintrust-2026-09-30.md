## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: 12 Hours Ago (as shown on posting page, 2026-09-30)

Advanced Software Engineer — Wintrust Financial Corporation

Wintrust provides community and commercial banking, specialty finance and wealth management services through its 16 bank charters and nine non-bank businesses. Wintrust is a $66 billion financial institution based in Rosemont, Illinois, and listed on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol "WTFC."

Why join us? An award-winning culture! Rated a Top Workplace by the Chicago Tribune (past 12 years). Competitive pay and discretionary or incentive bonus eligible. Comprehensive benefit package. Promote from within culture.

Position Overview: The Advanced Software Engineer is primarily responsible for the development and maintenance of Digital marketing tech stack. Collaborate in an Agile development environment to build, test, and integrate enterprise-grade components using AEM (Adobe Experience Manager) and Adobe Campaign. You will work closely with fellow engineers, designers, and system architects to deploy scalable digital experiences, construct data-driven workflows, and leverage broader MarTech tools within the Adobe Experience Cloud.

Participate in unit testing, quality assurance, and code deployments, while assisting with the triage of production issues.

This is a fully remote position. Please note that candidates who advance to the final stage of our interview process will be required to attend an in-person interview.

What You'll Do:
- Work with a team of technical resources focusing on development of marketing websites
- Works with systems managers and engineers, architects, business analysts and quality engineers in a highly collaborative environment to define product features, estimates and deliverables
- Assist in designing and building custom components, templates, and workflows in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Sites and Assets
- Help develop and configure audience segments, dynamic email templates, and automated customer journeys within Adobe Campaign
- Support integration between AEM, Adobe Campaign, and other Adobe Experience Cloud solutions (e.g., Adobe Target, Analytics, or Audience Manager)

Qualifications:
- Associate's degree or equivalent plus 3+ years' experience working with Adobe Digital Marketing technologies like AEM, Adobe Campaign, Adobe Analytics, Adobe Target, etc.
- Ability to manage multi-site networks (MSM), Working knowledge of HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), and HTL (Sightly) to construct and style reusable web components
- Experience in Adobe Campaign Classic building complex targeting workflows to filter, split, union, and enrich recipient profiles. Importing/exporting data, mapping external data sources, and managing List updates
- Familiarity with Java Content Repository (JCR), Apache Sling, and OSGi to integrate backend services
- Managing delivery execution, seeding, proofing, and scheduling in Adobe Campaign Classic

Benefits: Medical Insurance, Dental, Vision, Life insurance, AD&D, Short-term and long-term Disability Insurance, Parental Leave, EAP, Traditional and Roth 401(k) with company match, FSA, Employee Stock Purchase Plan at 5% discount, Critical Illness Insurance, Accident Insurance, Transportation and Commuting Benefits, Banking Benefits, Pet Insurance.

Compensation: The estimated salary range for this role is $59,000.00 - $77,000.00, along with eligibility to earn an annual bonus. Actual salaries may vary based on several factors, such as a candidate's qualifications, skills and experience.
