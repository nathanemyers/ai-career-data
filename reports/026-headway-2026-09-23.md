## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: not visible in source (pipeline entry recorded posted: 2026-08-10)

Staff Software Engineer - Experimentation

Location: Remote
Additional locations: San Francisco, CA | Seattle, WA | New York City
Type: FullTime
Work model: Remote

1 in 4 people in the US have a treatable mental health condition, but most providers don't accept insurance, making therapy too expensive for most people. Headway's mission is to fix this by building a new mental healthcare system everyone can access.

We're a Series D company with $325M+ in funding (a16z, Accel, Spark Capital, etc.).

ABOUT ENGINEERING AT HEADWAY

Principles: Focus on the mission. Ship small, learn fast. Everything is a product. Optimize for trust.

Tools we use: Python 3, TypeScript; FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, React, Remix; LaunchDarkly, Statsig, dbt, Snowflake; Postgres, Redis; AWS (Fargate, ECS, S3, and more), Cloudflare, Spark and Kafka; Datadog, PagerDuty, Sentry; Github.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Staff Software Engineer to own how Headway runs experiments. "Ship small, learn fast" is the second principle on the list above, and experimentation is how we actually do it: eleven teams have run hundreds of experiments deciding how patients find a therapist, how quickly new providers get up and running, whether our AI products are working, and how we collect the clinical outcomes data that proves care is effective. You will own the platform all of that runs on.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role with unusually wide reach. You will design and build the core primitives, including how users are assigned to experiments, how we record what someone actually saw, and how we recognize the same person across devices and between browsing anonymously and signing in. You will decide what our long-term experimentation and feature flagging stack should be; we run LaunchDarkly and Statsig today, and that architecture is yours to evaluate and consolidate. You will also make AI a real part of the workflow, from validating an experiment's setup before it starts to catching a broken experiment automatically.

You will also evolve our experimentation capabilities to expand into market-based testing. Headway is a multi-sided marketplace, so we frequently need to change something for therapists and measure what happens to patients. We need to test whole geographic markets, not just individual users. We need to hold groups out for long stretches to understand real long-term impact.

The job is a platform and a program in equal measure. Half of it is the technical foundation. The other half is raising how well the whole company experiments: the defaults, the review, the documentation, and the education that let a team run a trustworthy experiment without needing you in the room.

You will sit in the Patient org, where the highest volume of experiments happens, and serve the whole company. You will own assignment, exposure, and the experiment data pipelines. You will work closely with Data Science, who own statistical methodology, and with the teams who own our event tracking and metric definitions.

WHO YOU ARE

- You have owned an experimentation or feature flagging stack end to end rather than only used one.
- You have built the parts that do not come out of the box: assignment that persists correctly, exposure that reflects what a user actually saw, identity resolved across sessions and devices, or the diagnostics that catch a broken experiment early.
- You are fluent enough in the statistics to be a real partner to data scientists. You can tell the difference between a result that is weak and a result that is invalid.
- You have debugged the hard class of measurement bugs: traffic that split unevenly, users who ended up in both arms, effects that vanished once you looked at who was actually exposed.
- You have built a program and not only a platform. You have measurably raised how well an organization experiments through defaults, templates, review, and teaching.
- You have made a build versus buy call on a platform and lived with it.
- You have landed a migration on teams that did not ask for one, by making the new path easier than the old path rather than by mandate.
- You use AI as genuine leverage in your work and you have clear opinions about where it must not be trusted without a human.
- You have driven technical direction beyond your own team through design documents, reviews, and planning cycles, and you regularly mentor senior engineers.
- You are willing to flex across the stack. This role touches backend services, front end rendering, CDN behavior, and the data warehouse.
- Experience with multi-sided marketplaces, growth or search and ranking systems, or healthcare is a plus, though none is required.

Compensation and Benefits:

The expected base pay range for this position is $265,000-$331,000, based on a variety of factors including qualifications, experience, and geographic location. In addition to base salary, this role may be eligible for an equity grant.

Benefits offered include: Equity compensation, Medical/Dental/Vision, HSA/FSA, 401K, Work-from-Home Stipend, Therapy Reimbursement, 16-week parental leave, Carrot Fertility, 13 paid holidays, Flexible PTO, EAP, Training and professional development.
