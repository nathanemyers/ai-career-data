Software engineer 1 – AI/ML Engineer (Generative AI) — US Bank

At U.S. Bank, we're on a journey to do our best. Helping the customers and businesses we serve to make better and smarter financial decisions and enabling the communities we support to grow and succeed.

Job Description: "This position is not open to visa sponsorship"

This position will be responsible for the analysis, design, testing, development and maintenance of best in class software experiences. The candidate is a self-motivated individual who can collaborate with a team and across the organization.

Essential Responsibilities:
- Develop and maintain Generative AI applications using LLMs, RAG architectures, and prompt engineering techniques.
- Build and support AI workflows using frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, and Microsoft Foundry Agent Services.
- Collaborate with engineers and product teams to design, develop, test, and deploy AI-enabled solutions.
- Develop scalable applications and APIs using Python and microservices-based architectures.
- Assist with deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance optimization of AI applications in production environments.
- Support cloud-based AI solutions across Azure and AWS platforms while following security and operational best practices.
- Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, containerization, and infrastructure automation efforts.
- Participate in code reviews, documentation, and Agile delivery processes.

Basic Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree, or equivalent work experience
- Two to three years of relevant experience

Preferred Skills/Experience:
- Experience developing applications using Python and modern software engineering practices.
- Knowledge of Generative AI concepts, including Large Language Models (LLMs), Prompt Engineering, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Familiarity with AI orchestration frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, or Microsoft Foundry Agent Services.
- Experience with Azure and/or AWS cloud platforms and cloud-native application development.
- Understanding of REST APIs, microservices, distributed systems, and scalable application design.
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, ARM, or Bicep).
- Understanding of application monitoring, logging, troubleshooting, and production support practices.
- Strong collaboration, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Location expectations: This role requires working from a U.S. Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

Pay Range: $98,345.00 - $115,700.00. In addition to salary, U.S. Bank offers a comprehensive benefits package, including incentive and recognition programs, equity stock purchase 401(k) contribution and pension.

U.S. Bank participates in the E-Verify program. Posting may be closed earlier due to high volume of applicants.
