## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: not visible in source (pipeline entry recorded posted: 2025-07-11)

Staff Fullstack Software Engineer (Provider)

Location: New York, NY
Additional locations: San Francisco, CA | Seattle, WA | Remote
Type: FullTime

1 in 4 people in the US have a treatable mental health condition, but most providers don't accept insurance, making therapy too expensive for most people. Headway's mission is to fix this by building a new mental healthcare system everyone can access. We started by solving the biggest barrier to care: insurance. The admin work - credentialing, claims, payment reconciliation - is a nightmare. We've automated that.

But we're going further. Over 75,000 providers across all 50 states run their practice on our software, serving over 1 million patients. We are building the best tools for therapists to run their entire practice, reimagining the experience of finding a therapist, and investing in the platform foundations to enable this at scale. We aren't just a billing layer; we are becoming the platform where care actually happens.

We're a Series D company with $325M+ in funding (a16z, Accel, Spark Capital, etc.), looking for exceptional people to help us achieve this mission.

THE WORK

Fullstack at Headway means end-to-end ownership. You're building reliable abstraction layers over a fragmented healthcare system and translating them into seamless, empathetic user experiences. What you could work on:

- Therapist Practice Management: Build a best-in-class platform for Headway therapists to run their businesses. You'll own features from the database schema to the UI, creating intuitive web experiences for scheduling, AI-assisted clinical documentation, and payment management.
- Patient & Provider Engagement: Deepen the relationship between patients and their care. Build the interfaces and APIs that power seamless communication, personalized experiences, and tools that keep patients connected to their providers.
- The Modern AI-Enabled Clinical System: Help design and build the systems that manage clinical data. Create high-integrity experiences for treatment plans and progress notes, integrating AI-powered clinical intelligence.
- Frictionless Onboarding: Turn the historically painful process of insurance credentialing into a sleek, consumer-grade experience.
- Growing the Network: Own the tools and experiences that expand Headway's reach — from acquiring new providers to empowering existing ones.
- Technical foundations: Help the teams see around corners and lay the technical foundations for the future of our product.
- Strategic leadership: As a Staff Engineer, you will work with a cross functional leadership team to shape the roadmap and technical strategy for the space.

OUR STACK

- Languages: TypeScript, Python 3
- Frontend: React, Remix, Next.js
- Backend & Storage: FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Postgres, Redis, Snowflake
- Infrastructure: AWS, Temporal, Kafka
- Ops: Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty
- AI Tools: Cursor, Claude, Gemini, Eddy (our in-house cloud agent harness)

WHO YOU ARE

- End-to-End Product Builder: You care just as much about the API architecture as you do the user experience.
- Owns Outcomes, Not Tasks: You've been handed ambiguous problems and turned them into shipped software.
- Sets the Technical Standard: You make technical decisions that optimize for maintainability and scale.
- Mentor by Default: You pull engineers into design discussions without being asked.
- Ships Fast, Iterates Faster: You'd rather ship a good v1 this week than a perfect v1 next month.
- Quick Study in Complex Domains: You don't need prior healthcare experience, but you need to be someone who dives into unfamiliar domains and builds a working mental model fast.
- Motivated by Impact: You want your engineering skills applied to problems that matter.
- Communicates with Precision: Whether it's a Slack thread, a design doc, or a cross-team meeting.
- AI Frontier Tinkerer: You don't just use AI tools - you push their limits.

OUR INTERVIEW PROCESS

- Initial screen with recruiting.
- First round: live coding interview with Karat, Headway's technical interviewing partner.
- Final rounds: several team members, technical and non-technical.
- References and the Offer, including equity details.

COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

- Salary: $264,000 - $330,000 base salary plus a competitive equity package.
- Comprehensive Benefits: Medical, dental, vision, HSA/FSA, 401(k), Carrot Fertility reimbursement, and 16-week parental leave.
- Time Off: 13 holidays, a full week off in December, and flexible PTO.

Headway participates in E-Verify. A notice to Headway applicants: official communications come only from @findheadway.com.
