## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: Yesterday (as shown on posting page, 2026-09-30)

Software Engineer (Founding Engineer, Innovation Team) — Cerity Partners

Position Summary: As a founding engineer of the newly formed Innovation team, you will build and ship solutions with real complexity and scale to tens of thousands of clients. You will also play a key role in foundational decisions about architecture, design, systems, data, and product, wearing both Engineering and Product hats. This is a rare opportunity to build product at the 0 to 1 stage, and ship it immediately to scale. You will work directly with Will Peng, Chief Innovation Officer — a former founder of a fintech company that raised $40M and exited in 2025.

About Cerity Partners: Cerity Partners is one of the largest and fastest growing independent financial advisory firms, managing over $160+ billion in assets under management, and over $1 trillion assets under advisement. Backed by growth capital from Genstar Capital, Warburg Pincus, and Lightyear Capital, Cerity Partners ranks in the top 10 RIA's in the country by Barron's. Founded in 2009.

The Innovation Team at Cerity Partners: Cerity Partners' Innovation Team is the firm's center of excellence for the strategy and deployment of technology and applied AI. We believe the biggest opportunity is in the application of AI to scaled, complex industries like wealth and investment management. Our vision is to build the new OS of wealth intelligence. We are building the entire AI stack — harness, context, memory, evals, agents, models, workflows, data, knowledge.

Primary Responsibilities:
- Own the end-to-end experience: full user journeys and business goals, building solutions or partnering with the best vendor.
- Build in partnership with clients, advisors, and colleagues: identify high value opportunities, understand needs, map workflows, test prototypes.
- Establish foundational engineering principles and product strategy.
- Build at the cutting edge of applied AI: work across every part of the AI stack — harness, context, memory, evals, agents, models.

Required Qualifications:
- Minimum of 7 years of experience
- Financial services industry experience required - understanding of wealth management, investment advisory, or related financial services operations
- Demonstrated experience in AI tooling, models, harnesses, workflows
- Demonstrated ability to operate in ambiguity and building 0-to-1 products from first principles rather than established processes
- Expertise and experience with Claude Code, Codex, or similar

Skills and Competencies: track record of outsized impact; systems thinker; excellent product/business intuition; leads with agency; moves quickly with rigor; thrives in ambiguity; passionate about AI (experience building and shipping AI agents and harnesses); collaborative.

Compensation Range: $190,000 - $290,000 (salary & bonus)

Why Cerity Partners: Health, dental, vision insurance from day 1; 401(k) with 4% match; flexible PTO; parental leave; financial assistance for advanced education; commuter benefits.

Cerity Partners is an Equal Opportunity Employer. Applicants must be authorized to work for any employer in the U.S.
