### Gaps

1. **Tanstack + Tailwind (critical, Partial).** Not a hard blocker: both are libraries on top of React/TypeScript that a React engineer with 15 years picks up quickly, and Styled Components plus Redux are the direct analogs on record.
   - *Interview risk:* a screen asking "how would you structure server-state caching with TanStack Query" or "how do you manage a Tailwind design system at scale" exposes zero hands-on time.
   - *Mitigation:* before applying or before the first technical round, rebuild a small slice of Statz on Statz (or the AI-Assisted NBA Stats TypeScript project) with TanStack Query/Table and Tailwind so there is a real, honest reference; in the cover letter frame it as "Redux/Styled Components on record, TanStack/Tailwind ramp in progress". Never list them as skills until that work exists.
2. **Trading platform / real-time market data UI (critical + high, Partial).** Not a hard blocker; this is domain, not skill.
   - *Interview risk:* questions on rendering high-frequency ticks without jank (throttling, virtualization, WebSocket fan-out, memoization), order-entry correctness, and trader UX; no direct story.
   - *Mitigation:* lead with the GlobalNOC live-outage dashboard and the video editing suite (performance-sensitive rendering) as adjacent proof; prepare a whiteboard-level design for a real-time watchlist/order-book component. Mention TD Ameritrade honestly as brokerage-environment exposure, not trading UI.
