## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: Yesterday; Reposted Yesterday (as shown on posting page, 2026-09-30)

Software Engineer — Humana

Become a part of our caring community. Humana is seeking a Software Engineer 2 to help develop and support Salesforce Health Cloud solutions that power our growing Primary Care business. In this role, you will contribute to applications that enable sales teams to connect prospective patients with senior-focused primary care services.

Working alongside engineers, product owners, business analysts, and stakeholders, you will help design, build, test, and support Salesforce-based solutions while growing your expertise in Salesforce technologies, healthcare operations, and modern software engineering practices.

Work Style: Remote / Work at Home
Eligible Locations: Atlanta, GA; Boston, MA; Charlotte, NC; Chicago, IL; Dallas, TX; Washington, DC; Ft. Lauderdale, FL; Louisville, KY; Nashville, TN; New York, NY; Tampa, FL.

Key Responsibilities — Salesforce Development & Configuration:
- Develop and maintain Salesforce Health Cloud solutions using Apex, Flows, OmniStudio, and Lightning Components.
- Configure Salesforce functionality to support business requirements and enhance user experiences.
- Build and support custom workflows, automations, and platform-based solutions.
- Assist with integrations between Salesforce and enterprise applications using APIs and integration platforms.
- Participate in the design, testing, deployment, and support of new features and enhancements.
- Follow established engineering standards, coding best practices, and security requirements.

Collaboration & Agile Delivery:
- Partner with product owners, business analysts, and stakeholders to understand business needs.
- Collaborate across Sales, Operations, Marketing, and IT teams.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, daily standups, backlog refinement, and retrospectives.
- Contribute to peer code reviews, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Testing & Support:
- Develop and execute unit tests and support integration and user acceptance testing.
- Troubleshoot application issues and production defects.
- Support application deployments and post-release validation activities.
- Participate in root cause analysis and implementation of corrective actions.

Documentation:
- Maintain user stories, technical tasks, and development activities in Azure DevOps (ADO).
- Create and update technical, configuration, and support documentation.

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
- 2+ years of software engineering or application development experience.
- 2+ years of Salesforce development experience, including Apex, Flows, Lightning Components, or OmniStudio.
- Experience with Salesforce Sales Cloud; Health Cloud experience is a plus.
- Experience working with APIs, integrations, or connected enterprise applications.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and software development lifecycle (SDLC) practices.
- Experience leveraging AI-powered tools to improve development productivity, testing, troubleshooting, and documentation.
- Understanding of modern AI concepts such as Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agentic development.
- Successful completion of Humana's AI skills assessment.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Salesforce Certified Associate, Salesforce Administrator, or Platform App Builder certification.
- Experience with Salesforce Health Cloud.
- Experience integrating Salesforce with external systems using APIs or MuleSoft.
- Experience with JavaScript, web technologies, and modern UI frameworks.
- Experience using Azure DevOps, Git, and CI/CD tools.
- Experience working in healthcare, health insurance, or other regulated industries.

Work at Home Requirements: minimum download speed of 25 Mbps and upload speed of 10 Mbps; dedicated space lacking ongoing interruptions to protect member PHI/HIPAA information. Occasional travel to Humana's offices for training or meetings may be required.

Scheduled Weekly Hours: 40

Pay Range: $80,900 - $110,300 per year. This job is eligible for a bonus incentive plan based upon company and/or individual performance.

About Humana: Humana Inc. (NYSE: HUM) is a leading U.S. healthcare company.
