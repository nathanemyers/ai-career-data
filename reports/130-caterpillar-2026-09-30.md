## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: 2 Days Ago (as shown on posting page, 2026-09-30)

PIM Platform Engineering Manager - Software Engineering — Caterpillar

Career Area: Technology, Digital and Data

Your Work Shapes the World at Caterpillar Inc. Cat Digital is the digital and technology arm of Caterpillar Inc., leveraging the latest technologies to build industry-leading digital solutions for our customers and dealers. With over 1.5 million connected assets worldwide, our teams use data, technology, advanced analytics, and AI capabilities to help our customers build a better, more sustainable world.

Job Summary: The PIM Platform Engineering Manager leads engineering strategy and delivery for the PIM platform, owning technical direction, architecture review, and quality/reliability for the team's Akeneo and Informatica P360 migration work. This is a hands-on leadership role: you set technical direction, unblock delivery, and are the final technical decision-maker for the platform.

What You Will Do:
- Lead technical direction and platform engineering strategy for Akeneo, including architecture review and technical decision-making
- Own delivery execution and backlog shaping across the PIM engineering team (tech leads, engineers, BA, TPO, sys admin)
- Guide the Informatica P360-to-Akeneo migration and transition, including migration planning and integration strategy
- Review solution designs and ensure workflows, enrichment rules, and Akeneo extensions are built on reusable, standardized patterns
- Validate technical readiness for releases and migration cutovers; own quality and reliability for the platform
- Provide vendor/SOW oversight (Capgemini) for P360 maintenance during the Akeneo transition
- Review backlog quality and ensure the team consistently ships working platform capabilities
- Solve hands-on technical problems and unblock integration/migration issues directly when needed

What You Will Have: Products and Services; Organizational Leadership; Software Development; Software Development Life Cycle; Software Product Design/Architecture; Software Quality Assurance and Testing.

Considerations For Top Candidates:
- Proven engineering leadership experience managing platform, PIM, or MDM engineering teams
- Hands-on technical background in Akeneo
- Strong architecture and solution-design review experience, including API and integration design
- Experience leading platform migrations (system-to-system data and workflow migration)
- Comfortable owning delivery execution: backlog management, sprint planning, release readiness
- Vendor/SOW management experience
- Strong communicator who can align architecture, business, and delivery teams

Additional Details:
- This position requires the candidate to be in Chicago, IL, Peoria, IL, Broomfield, CO or Irving, TX
- Relocation assistance is NOT available for this position
- Visa sponsorship is NOT available with this position
- This position will require up to 10% travel

Summary Pay Range: $147,760.00 - $240,110.00. Compensation and benefits offered may vary depending on multiple individualized factors.

Benefits: Medical/dental/vision; PTO; 401(k); HSA; FSAs; Health Lifestyle Programs; EAP; Voluntary Benefits and Employee Discounts; Career Development; Incentive bonus; Disability benefits; Life Insurance; Parental leave; Adoption benefits; Tuition Reimbursement.

Posting Dates: September 27, 2026 - October 5, 2026. Any offer of employment is conditioned upon the successful completion of a drug screen.

Caterpillar is an Equal Opportunity Employer, Including Veterans and Individuals with Disabilities.
